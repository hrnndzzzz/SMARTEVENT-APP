## If a caption has to be cut

Keep **3, 16, 17, 23 and 27**. Those answer the reviews directly: the
status detail, the form our professor called cluttered, the office
being given something to read rather than a form to fill in, and —
new since the last cut of this list — a returned proposal arriving
with the reason attached.

Then **11, 20 and 22**, which are the moderator's enhancements most
visible on camera: a report per event, the paper's own budget columns,
and one proposal in the queue at a time.

Drop **4, 5, 7, 12, 14, 18, 21** first. They are real, and none of
them is what either review asked about.

## Not filmable

**Anything that needs the server.** Submitting, SDS signing and
printing all go through the backend, which renders the SAP form
through Microsoft Word on Windows. The demo runs offline on seeded
data, so the walkthrough above stops at the edge of every one of
those. If the recording is meant to show a real submission, the
backend has to be running on a signed-in Windows session first —
see `smartevent-backend/SETUP.md`.
