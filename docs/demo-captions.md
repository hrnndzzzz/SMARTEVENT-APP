# Demo video: captions

One caption per feature, in the order the walkthrough runs them.
No narration — these are the on-screen texts.

| # | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome, holding the heading | **Demo mode** — hold the heading, no account needed |
| 2 | The demo dialog | **Starts as a treasurer** — every role switchable in Settings |
| 3 | Dashboard, the event cards | **Each proposal says which desk has it** — with the moderator, with the SDS Office, with the signatories |
| 4 | The planner sheet | **The date opens a planner** — everything with a date on it |
| 5 | The account menu | **The role line opens your account** |
| 6 | Money | **Budget at a glance** — green while there is room, amber as it tightens, red when it is nearly gone |
| 7 | Reports, the chart grid | **Four charts** — tap any one to open it full screen |
| 8 | The download window | **One button to export** — PDF, CSV or print-ready |
| 9 | Search results | **Search finds screens, not just records** — "receipt" opens the scanner |
| 10 | The proposal cover | **The form opens on its cover** — dates filled in, off-campus asked first |
| 11 | Part 1 | **Twenty fields, six groups** — colour-coded by what they are about |
| 12 | Swiping to Support | **Swipe between the three steps** |
| 13 | The support grid | **School support as a grid** — detail appears only for what you tick |
| 14 | Settings, the role picker | **Switch role without signing out** — same records, different reader |
| 15 | The SDS summary | **The SDS Office reads a summary**, not the form the proposer fills in |
| 16 | The signature pad | **One electronic signature** — the nine on paper stay paper |

## If a caption has to be cut

Keep 3, 10, 11 and 15. Those are the four our professor's review asked
for directly: the status detail, the cluttered form, and the office
being given something to read rather than a form to fill in.
