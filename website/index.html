Inventory, money, events and reports.
One phone.

Student organizations run on spreadsheets, paper and Messenger. Records go missing, budgets are unclear, equipment is untracked, and every turnover of officers starts again from nothing. SMARTEVENT puts all of it in one place, on the device officers already carry.

What it does

Eight things, properly

Every one of these is built and running. Nothing here is planned.

01

Event proposals

Submit a proposal, follow every approval step, and generate the signed SAP form as a PDF.

02

Money and budgets

Record expenses and income. Balances deduct themselves, so nobody is adding up receipts by hand.

03

Receipt scanning

Photograph a receipt and the app reads the vendor, the date and the amount off it.

04

Inventory

Stock, movements and low-stock items, grouped by where each thing is kept.

05

Reports and exports

Financial reports as PDF, CSV or print-ready, plus Excel exports for anyone who wants the rows.

06

AI assistance

A budget suggested from similar past events, a category suggested for a scanned receipt, and a plain-language reading of the report. Every one is a suggestion: nothing is saved until a person acts on it.

07

Works offline

Record expenses and movements without signal. Everything syncs when the phone reconnects.

08

Role-based access

Officers, advisers, the SDS Office and administrators each see only what their role allows.

Try it

Look around without an account

The app carries a demo inside it. It runs on seeded records with no server behind it, so you can open every screen, propose an event and record an expense without touching anybody's real data — and without waiting to be approved.

Step 01

Install it

Request the APK below. The demo is in every build; there is no separate one to download.

Step 02

Open Sign in

From the welcome screen, tap Sign in. Do not type anything.

Step 03

Hold the heading

Press and hold Welcome back for a second. A dialog offers demo mode.

Step 04

Enter demo

You start as a treasurer. Settings has a role picker for the officer, the adviser, the SDS Office and the administrators.

What the demo does and does not do. Nothing leaves the phone: there is no server call, so exports and anything else needing the backend say so rather than pretending. The records are seeded and reset with the app. Signing in with a real account is a different thing entirely, and the demo holds no session.

Who it is for

Four roles, one record

Everyone reads the same figures. Nobody sees more than their role allows.

Student organization officers

Presidents, treasurers and inventory officers, who keep the records, the budgets and the events.

Faculty moderators and advisers

Review event proposals and budget requests, watch balances, and read the reports and analytics.

Student Development Services

Review submitted proposals, sign off on the SAP form, and monitor events across organizations.

System administrators

Configure the system and manage who may hold an account.

Get the app

Request access

SMARTEVENT is not on the Play Store. It is distributed to the student organizations of the LCUP CITE Department, so the download goes out by request. Fill this in and an administrator will review it; if it is approved, the link is emailed to the address you give here.

What happens to what you type. The request is held until an administrator reviews it, and the email address is used to send you the link and nothing else. Approval is a person's decision, not an automatic one, so it will not be instant.
